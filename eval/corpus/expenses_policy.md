# Northwind Logistics — Expenses Policy (Synthetic)

Invented for evaluation purposes. No real company, rate or supplier is described here.

## Submitting a claim

Expense claims must be submitted within sixty days of the date on the receipt. Claims
submitted after sixty days are rejected automatically and require a written exception from a
finance manager. Every claim over 25 pounds needs an itemised receipt; a card statement is not
accepted as a receipt.

## Approval thresholds

Claims up to 250 pounds are approved by a line manager. Claims between 250 and 2,000 pounds
need a department head. Claims above 2,000 pounds need both a department head and the finance
director. No employee may approve their own claim, regardless of amount.

## Travel

Standard class rail travel is reimbursed in full. First class rail is reimbursed only when
booked more than fourteen days in advance and when it is cheaper than the standard class fare
on the day. Economy air travel is reimbursed for flights under six hours; premium economy is
permitted for flights over six hours.

Mileage in a private vehicle is reimbursed at 45 pence per mile for the first ten thousand
miles in a tax year, and 25 pence per mile thereafter.

## Accommodation and meals

Accommodation is reimbursed up to 150 pounds per night in London and 110 pounds per night
elsewhere in the United Kingdom. The meal allowance is 40 pounds per day when travelling
domestically and 60 pounds per day internationally. Alcohol is not reimbursable under any
circumstances.

## Non-reimbursable items

The following are never reimbursed: parking fines and traffic penalties, personal
entertainment, hotel minibar charges, flight upgrades paid at the gate, and loss of personal
property.
