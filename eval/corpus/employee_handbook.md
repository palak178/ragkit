# Northwind Logistics — Employee Handbook (Synthetic)

This document is invented for evaluation purposes. Northwind Logistics does not exist, and
none of the policies below describe any real organisation.

## Notice and resignation

Employees must give thirty days written notice before resigning. The notice period begins on
the date the written resignation is received by the people team, not the date it was signed.
Employees still inside their probation period give seven days notice instead of thirty.

## Probation

The probation period lasts six months from the start date. A probation review happens at the
third month and again at the fifth month. Probation may be extended once, by a maximum of
three months, and only with written approval from a department head.

## Annual leave

Full-time employees accrue twenty-one days of paid annual leave each calendar year, accruing
at one and three quarter days per completed month. Part-time employees accrue leave in
proportion to contracted hours. Unused annual leave expires on 31 March of the following year
and is not paid out on departure.

## Sick leave

Employees receive ten days of paid sick leave per calendar year. A medical certificate is
required for any absence longer than three consecutive working days. Sick leave does not
carry over between years.

## Parental leave

Primary caregivers receive twenty-six weeks of paid parental leave. Secondary caregivers
receive eight weeks. Both must give at least ten weeks notice before the intended start date.

## Remote work

Employees may work remotely up to three days per week by default. Fully remote arrangements
require written approval from a department head and are reviewed every twelve months.
Employees working remotely must be reachable during the core hours of 10:00 to 16:00 in their
registered time zone.

## Equipment

Company laptops must be returned on the final working day. A replacement fee of 400 pounds
applies to equipment that is not returned within fourteen days of departure. Monitors,
keyboards and headsets under 50 pounds in value do not need to be returned.
